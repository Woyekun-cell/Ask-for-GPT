# Ask for GPT — full feature walkthrough

English synthetic narration. macOS overview.

## 00:00 Meet Ask for GPT

Meet Ask for GPT, a reading and writing companion that brings word lookup, translation, questions, and editing into the tools you already use.

## 00:11 Look up words and translate passages

Start with a word. Select it and open the reading panel for pronunciation and meanings from the local dictionary. For a passage, choose Local for Apple translation on supported macOS versions, GPT for a closer translation, or Explain to explore terminology. You can also paste text and change the translation direction.

## 00:34 Ask with the passage attached

Have a specific question? Bring the selected passage into Ask GPT and continue the conversation with its context attached. When supporting material is needed, questions can use web search and clickable source links.

## 00:50 Follow translations on the original page

In Chrome, paragraph translations appear beside the original page. Translation follows your reading position, with controls to pause, resume, change colors, or restore the original. You can work through a page while keeping the source material in view.

## 01:08 Read PDFs in two languages

In Zotero, start with one page or translate the document. Read the original, the translation, or both side by side while preserving the PDF layout. Existing completed translations can be reused when you return, and the original attachment remains the basis of your reading.

## 01:27 Inspect correspondences and keep notes

Select a word or phrase to inspect its established correspondence across languages. Zotero's native color palette lets you save your own highlights and underlines. Keep annotations and comments with the paper, and return to them as you read. Ordinary selection does not automatically save a mark.

## 01:47 Choose how the panes move

Keep the panes linked while reading, or move them independently when comparing different sections. Shared themes and Fit Width keep both sides comfortable to read. The short recordings here show the current reading controls, with existing highlights retained.

## 02:07 Find a figure and inspect the details

Find a figure or table by its caption, then jump to its location. Open an image in a separate preview, zoom into details, fit it to the window, or export a PNG. Keep the figure visible while you compare it with the results text.

## 02:24 Review writing suggestions before applying

When it is time to write, compare the original wording with a spelling or writing suggestion before deciding what to apply. Word offers inline suggestions, while WPS uses explicit sidebar checks. Review suggestions in context and keep control of your final wording.

## 02:43 Read, compare, ask and revise

The local dictionary works offline. In this Mac build, AI features use your own Codex connection. Read, compare, ask, and revise, with your original documents and reading notes kept in place. Download and setup instructions are in the README.
